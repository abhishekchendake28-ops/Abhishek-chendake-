ABOUT THE AUTHOR

About Me

The person behind the thoughts, questions and words.

Abhishek Sanjay Chendake

I am a writer, philosopher and creator from Mulegaon, Solapur, Maharashtra, India. My work is deeply connected with philosophy, human thoughts, existence, truth, self-discovery and the continuous search for a deeper understanding of life.

Writing, for me, is not simply about putting words on a page. It is a way of exploring questions, observing life and understanding the thoughts that exist within and around us.

My Way of Thinking

Human life is filled with questions. Some questions have clear answers, while others remain open throughout our lives. I am particularly interested in questions that make us pause and look beyond ordinary assumptions.

Who are we? Why do we think the way we think? What is truth? What gives life meaning? How much of our identity is created by our own understanding, and how much is shaped by the world around us?

These questions form an important part of my writing. I see philosophy as a way of observing life, questioning ideas and developing a deeper awareness of ourselves.

My Writing

My writing explores the relationship between thoughts, emotions, identity, existence, perception and personal growth. I try to approach these subjects in a way that allows readers to connect philosophical ideas with their own experiences.

A simple thought can sometimes lead to a much larger question. That question can become the beginning of reflection, and reflection can become the beginning of self-discovery.

Through my books and articles, I want to create a space where readers can slow down, think deeply and develop their own perspectives.

My Vision

My vision is to continue writing, learning and exploring ideas that encourage curiosity, self-reflection and deeper thinking.

I want my work to encourage readers to question assumptions, look within themselves and explore life from different perspectives.

I do not aim to provide a final answer to every philosophical question. Instead, I want my writing to create a starting point for thought and reflection.

“Sometimes the beginning of understanding is simply having the courage to ask a question.”

My Roots
