About Me

Abhishek Sanjay Chendake

My name is Abhishek Sanjay Chendake, a writer, philosopher and creator from Mulegaon, Solapur, Maharashtra, India. My work is deeply connected with philosophy, human thoughts, existence, truth, self-discovery and the continuous search for a deeper understanding of life.

I have always been fascinated by the questions that exist beneath ordinary life. Questions such as: Who are we? Why do we think the way we think? What gives life meaning? What is truth? How much of what we believe is actually our own understanding, and how much is shaped by the world around us?

For me, philosophy is not limited to books or academic ideas. It is a way of looking at life. It is the habit of questioning, observing, reflecting and trying to understand ourselves and the world with greater clarity.

Through my writing, I try to explore these ideas in a simple and thoughtful way so that readers can connect them with their own lives and experiences.

My writing focuses on the relationship between the human mind, thoughts, emotions, identity, existence and personal growth. I am interested in the deeper questions that often remain hidden behind our everyday experiences.

Sometimes a simple thought can lead to a much larger question, and that question can become the beginning of self-discovery. I believe that honest questioning can open new ways of looking at ourselves and the world.

My books are an extension of this curiosity. Through them, I explore different dimensions of thought, life and self-understanding.

My aim is not to provide a final answer to every philosophical question, but to create a space where readers can pause, think and discover their own perspectives.

My Books

I have written three books: The Ocean of Thoughts, अज्ञाताचा शोध and The Better Version of You. Each book explores different aspects of thoughts, philosophy, self-discovery, personal growth and the human experience.
